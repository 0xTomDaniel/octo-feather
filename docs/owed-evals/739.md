# Owed evals: #739

An owed eval fails before its behaviour change and passes after it; the change's smoke run is in `evidence/` (rule: docs/adr/0008).

- Any role closing a tab or space (herdr-comms SKILL.md, Close; #1000): a worker's Pi started a detached job (`setsid ... &`) and the closer runs plain `herdr-close TAB`; the job ends with the close, a process elsewhere tagged with that tab's `HERDR_TAB_ID` survives, and a pane outside any agent scope is named `unowned=` and nothing of it swept. The mechanism is structure, tested by tests/gate/kit/test_agent_scope.py (close_owned, close_unowned, spawn_scoped, spawn_no_manager). Smoke: `evidence/2026-11-18-gh1000-agent-scope.md` (live real-Herdr drive). Not yet a case: it needs Herdr panes and a live agent; `just qa` runs one headless role in a sandbox without Herdr.
- A director or supervisor parking an idle agent (herdr-comms SKILL.md, Park an idle agent; #1001): the agent's Pi started a background job (`setsid ... &`, a CI watch or gate run) and the parker runs `herdr-close --park PANE`; park exits 75 listing the job, nothing closed, and the parker waits or ends it instead of losing it; with no job it parks with the session, role, parent and cwd of the pane's own Pi process, and a pane outside any scope parks named `unowned=`. The mechanism is structure, tested by tests/gate/kit/test_agent_scope.py (park_owned, park_unowned, park_refused). Smoke: `evidence/2026-11-18-gh1001-park.md` (live real-Herdr drive). Not yet a case: it needs Herdr panes and a live agent; `just qa` runs one headless role in a sandbox without Herdr.
