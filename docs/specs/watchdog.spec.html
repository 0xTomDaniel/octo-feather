Watchdog

docs / specs / watchdog.spec.html

An independent scheduled backstop: deterministic delivery wakes Luna; Luna judges actual progress; the director intervenes within existing authority.

Session scope and messaging: comms. Agent launch: roles. Owner gates: lifecycle.

User stories

As the owner, I have scheduled watchdog coverage even when the director and supervisors are idle and no callback arrives.

As the director, I receive evidence-backed findings about unfinished mandates and lost results without legitimate waits being treated as stalls.

As the director, I receive an evidence-backed escalation when a claimed current baseline contradicts actual remote source facts, without every behind-main branch being treated as unhealthy.

As the owner, I can distinguish demonstrated monitoring from a configured timer, an idle agent, or failed delivery.

Acceptance criteria

Given the director with an enabled watchdog and idle supervisors receiving no factory events, When the director leaves the factory idle through a configured schedule interval, Then watchdog receives a scheduled wake and returns a Luna assessment independently of the director's activity.

Given the director with an idle supervisor awaiting a completed job whose callback was not delivered, needs an induced failure: callback delivery fails, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then the director receives an escalation identifying the lane, the completed result, the missed callback, elapsed context and a suggested intervention.

Given the director with a supervisor idle on an unfinished mandate and no supporting wait evidence, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then the director receives an escalation identifying the unfinished outcome, the evidence and elapsed context, and a suggested intervention.

Given the director with a tool claimed to use the current baseline while its actual remotely installed source contradicts that claim, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then the director receives an escalation identifying the affected tool, the claimed baseline, the actual remote source, the conflicting evidence and elapsed context, and a suggested intervention.

Given the director with a lane intentionally working on a branch behind main whose source matches its declared baseline and whose mandate is advancing, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then watchdog records the matching baseline and progress without issuing an alert solely because the branch is behind main.

Given the director with a lane producing the outcomes its mandate calls for, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then watchdog records an assessment of progress supported by those outcomes without issuing a stall alert for the lane.

Given the director with a lane legitimately waiting at an unanswered owner gate, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then watchdog records the owner gate and its elapsed context as a legitimate wait without issuing a stall alert solely for silence.

Given the director with a quiet lane awaiting a verified long-running job, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then watchdog records the job evidence and expected outcome as a legitimate wait without issuing a stall alert solely for silence.

Given the director with an unresolved watchdog finding and unchanged supporting evidence, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through another configured schedule interval, Then watchdog records the repeated assessment without delivering a duplicate alert.

Given the director with an unresolved watchdog finding whose evidence materially changes, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through another configured schedule interval, Then the director receives an updated finding that identifies the new evidence.

Given the director with an enabled watchdog configured for its required model and thinking level, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then the completed assessment identifies the actual runtime model and thinking level as matching watchdog's required configuration.

Given the director with an enabled watchdog whose agent cannot receive a prompt, needs an induced failure: watchdog prompt delivery fails, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then watchdog coverage shows a failed wake with its time and reason, rather than a completed assessment.

Given the director with a watchdog finding whose escalation cannot be delivered, needs an induced failure: director prompt delivery fails, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then watchdog coverage shows the undelivered finding with its time and delivery failure, rather than a delivered escalation.

Given the director with an enabled watchdog already assessing the factory, When the director leaves the assessment running through the next configured schedule interval, Then watchdog coverage identifies the ongoing assessment without starting a concurrent assessment.

Given the director with a retained undelivered watchdog finding and restored director reachability, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through another configured schedule interval, Then the director receives the retained finding and watchdog coverage records successful delivery without losing the preceding failure.

Given the director with a retained failed watchdog wake and restored watchdog reachability, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through another configured schedule interval, Then watchdog completes a scheduled assessment and coverage preserves the preceding failed wake.

Given the director with an enabled watchdog whose setup chat has closed, When the director leaves the factory enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then watchdog completes a scheduled assessment under that factory session's ownership independently of the closed chat.

Given the director with an enabled watchdog, When the director explicitly shuts down the factory session then waits through the next configured schedule interval, Then coverage shows the session's schedule disabled and no new watchdog assessment starts for that interval.

Given the director with an enabled watchdog and idle supervisors, When the director leaves watchdog enabled through a configured schedule interval, Then the scheduler delivers only the observational watchdog wake without launching a director sweep, creating agents, directing lane work or advancing tracker state.

Modular boundaries

The scheduler owns independent wake delivery, bounded execution, overlap control and visible execution failures, never factory-health classification. Caller-facing seam: enabling and disabling a session's schedule and inspecting its scheduled runs, wake outcomes and delivery failures. Dependency direction: external-provider; the scheduler invokes the existing agent transport in the explicitly selected factory session. Observable scope: scheduled wakes, actual responses, non-overlapping runs and coverage failures.

Luna owns judgment from source evidence and the resulting finding. Caller-facing seam: a scheduled observation mandate and an evidence-backed assessment or escalation. Dependency direction: external-provider; Luna reads factory evidence and reports through scoped Herdr transport to the director, never to workers. Observable scope: assessments and delivered findings, not changes to lane work.

The director owns consuming findings and unblocking or redirecting supervisors within existing authority. Caller-facing seam: delivered watchdog findings through the director's session-scoped message channel. Dependency direction: external-provider; the director consumes watchdog output but its cooperation and relays are never prerequisites for scheduled wakes. Observable scope: director intervention and escalation through existing gates.

Evidence, not activity

Each scheduled observation follows mandates to outcomes
EvidenceJudgment it supportsInsufficient by itself
Supervisor dialogue, unfinished mandates and durable handoffsWhether the requested outcome is advancingRepeated planning, process existence, output volume or Herdr working state
Expected results, actual job receipts and callback outcomesWhether a result exists but has not reached its supervisorA live process or an assertion that a callback should arrive
Claimed current baseline, declared target baseline and actual remotely installed or running source factsWhether a current-baseline claim contradicts the actual tool or deploymentBeing behind main alone; a branch's revision without its declared target and outcome context
Owner gates, dependency gates and verified running jobsWhether silence reflects a legitimate waitElapsed silence alone

Watchdog observes supervisors one layer below the director across that session's target repositories. It does not take over worker management.

Watchdog's required runtime is openai/gpt-6-luna at xhigh. A configured value alone does not establish the runtime actually used. An implicit default-model resume is not permitted.

Watchdog is observational: no implementation, merges, owner acceptance, traffic shifts or bypassing gates. Its observational contract is trusted policy, not a claimed technical sandbox; Bash and codemode do not become read-only by omitting edit and write tools.

A configured timer and a live or idle agent are not operational proof. Coverage distinguishes scheduled trigger, actual wake, completed response and delivered escalation, with times and failures visible.

Schedule and ownership

A maintained systemd user timer schedules watchdog every five minutes, independently of factory activity. This is a watchdog-only observational scheduling exception, not a workflow engine, tracker-driven state machine or director sweep launcher; see ADR 0026.

Each enabled factory session owns its recurring watchdog schedule until explicit factory shutdown, independently of shaping or research chats. No automatic extra-agent creation or default-model fallback is permitted; missing agents, wrong configuration and resource pressure are visible coverage failures. Schedule execution and reporting preserve the explicitly selected factory session and parent authority.

Failed wakes and undelivered findings remain visibly unresolved and are retried on subsequent scheduled runs without bypassing the director to contact the owner. A failed delivery remains eligible for retry even when its finding is unchanged; duplicate suppression applies to findings already delivered, not attempts. An attempted notification is not delivered escalation.
