# The director delegates finite non-lane work to general-purpose workers

A director required to delegate substantive work needs an executor for tasks that do not belong to a product lane. Use one reusable general-worker role through the existing controlled launcher, with ordinary task briefs, rather than a separate launch mode or specialist for every chore: see [general-purpose workers](../specs/roles.spec.html#general-purpose-workers) and [parent routing](../specs/roles.spec.html#acceptance-task-worker-route). This supersedes ADR [0015](0015-escalations-go-up-the-chain.md) only where it requires an intervening supervisor for the director's own finite non-lane assignments; product implementation remains supervisor-owned.

A cleanup-only exception leaves the next unrelated task blocked; direct execution by the coordinator defeats delegation. A normal role preserves the role-file and launcher decisions in ADRs 0013 and 0016 while allowing the director to retain context for decisions rather than substantive execution. Supervisors can use that same general role for scoped research and diagnosis without replacing their implementation and independent-review workers.
