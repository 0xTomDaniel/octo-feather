## Could qa.sh hang unbounded? Checked, no

Every octo-feather-qa call in tests/qa.sh goes through `run`, `timeout -k 5 240`. Its `$(...)` pipe is held only by
timeout's process group: the sandbox (`process_group=0`) gets fresh pipes (stdin /dev/null, stdout and stderr pipes to qa,
pass_fds the proof/post/err pipes; `ls -l /proc/<sandbox>/fd` during a run: no fd on qa.sh's pipe). Forced: a stub pi
that sleeps 1000 s under a case with timeout_s 600, run as `run` does with a 15 s bound: `rc=124 after 15 s`. The other
qa.sh commands (check-evals, python heredocs, git, tar, jq) read files only.

Observed, not changed (outside the cause): when that bound fires, qa dies (no SIGTERM handler) and its sandbox, in its
own process group, outlives it until the watcher's own timeout_s (the forced run left pid 1308673 running; killed by hand).
It holds none of qa.sh's fds, so it never blocks qa.sh. octo-feather-qc kills its sandbox group on SIGTERM; octo-feather-qa
does not. A ticket if wanted.
